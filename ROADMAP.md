# Roadmap

This document tracks where **Liepin Auto-Apply Wizard** is headed. It is intentionally
lightweight - the best way to influence direction is to open a
[Discussion](https://github.com/FrankHu-HK/liepin-auto-apply-wizard/discussions) or a
[feature request](https://github.com/FrankHu-HK/liepin-auto-apply-wizard/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

## Planned
- Broaden language coverage and localization of in-product copy.
- More worked examples and end-to-end walkthroughs.
- Richer evaluation / self-check reports.

## In Progress
- Hardening the local-first pipelines and error messages.
- Clearer "what changed / why" notes per release.

## Under Consideration
- Integrations suggested by the community (see Discussions).
- Optional cloud fallbacks that stay opt-in and clearly labeled.

Want something else? Open a Discussion - popular asks get promoted here.
